About

A little about how I work.

I'm a UI/UX designer and frontend developer based in Ahmedabad, India. I enjoy turning requirements into clean, well-structured screens — starting from user flows and wireframes, then refining layouts until they feel simple and easy to use.

Alongside design, I build the front end of the interfaces I design, translating layouts into responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I've also worked with AI and machine learning fundamentals, including practical exposure to Python, OpenCV, and Microsoft Copilot, which shapes how I think about smarter, more useful interfaces.

I work well in teams, pick up new tools quickly, and care about getting the small details — spacing, hierarchy, consistency — right.
